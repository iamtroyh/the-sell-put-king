International Business Machines Corporation (NYSE: IBM) has successfully transformed into a hybrid cloud and enterprise AI powerhouse, driven by Red Hat OpenShift, IBM Software, and watsonx orchestration platforms. Following a ~35.9% pullback from its 52-week high of $332.46, IBM stock trades near critical long-term structural support at $213.00 (RSI: 27.27 oversold).
