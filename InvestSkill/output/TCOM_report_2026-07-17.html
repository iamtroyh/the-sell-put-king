Trip.com Group Limited (NASDAQ: TCOM) is the dominant Online Travel Agency (OTA) in China and Asia-Pacific (operating Ctrip, Qunar, Trip.com, and Skyscanner). The stock has experienced a -46.3% price reset from its 52-week peak of $78.99 down to $42.45, driven by broader Chinese market multiple compression.

Operationally, Trip.com commands exceptional margins (80.3% Gross Margin, 24.3% Operating Margin) and holds a massive net cash balance of ~$6.9B USD (accounting for ~25% of its entire market cap). With Trailing P/E at an ultra-low 6.38x and Wall Street target standing at $60.62 (+42.8% upside), TCOM represents a classic deep value opportunity.
