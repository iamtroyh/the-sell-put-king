2. 分析章节 2
InvestSkill 多因子投研 - INTU
SEC-02

Intuit Inc. (NASDAQ: INTU) is the undisputed industry titan in financial management, compliance, small business accounting, and automated marketing software. Powering mission-critical platforms such as QuickBooks (>80% US market share), TurboTax (>60% DIY tax software share), Mailchimp, Credit Karma, and ProConnect, Intuit commands immense switching costs and high customer retention across 100M+ global users.

Following a severe macro selloff that dragged INTU down 64.4% from its 52-week peak of $813.70 to $289.99, Intuit is trading at an unprecedented valuation discount. At 10.61x Forward P/E (vs. historical multi-year average of 35x–45x), 0.76x PEG Ratio, and an attractive 6.6% Free Cash Flow yield ($5.23B FCF), Intuit represents a rare, blue-chip Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) monopoly opportunity for disciplined long-term investors.
